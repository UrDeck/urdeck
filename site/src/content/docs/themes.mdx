A theme decides how every card on a page looks: its colours, corners, border, light and shadow, the fonts, the shape of gauges,
the page behind the cards and a little motion. Widgets only draw their content, so a theme changes all of them at once and
a new widget needs no styling of its own.

Choose one with the `theme` key of `urdeck-config.json`, for example `"theme": "carbon"`. Saving the file applies it; no
restart. The picker in the corner of this site shows every picture on the page in the theme you choose.

## The built-in themes

Each is drawn here from the sample page by UrDeck itself, over the background it names. Most name a [background](/docs/backgrounds/)
that suits them and fall back to a still colour or gradient of their own where shaders are unavailable.

`off` it stands still.

## Your own

A theme is a folder next to `UrDeck.Host.exe`: `themes/my-theme/theme.json`, and font files if it uses any. Every value is
optional and what you leave out comes from `default-dark`, so a theme can be a few lines.

- [A theme in five minutes](/docs/themes/quick-start/) makes one, from nothing or from a copy of a built-in theme.
- [The theme reference](/docs/themes/reference/) lists every key with its range and default.
- [The cookbook](/docs/cookbook/) has recipes for chamfered cards, a gradient border, a hard shadow, LED bars and more, each a
  small theme you can copy.
- [Fonts and licences](/docs/themes/fonts/), [icon sets](/docs/themes/icons/) and [motion](/docs/themes/motion/) have a page each.
- [Checking a theme](/docs/themes/checking/) finds mistakes and draws a picture of the theme without a deck.
