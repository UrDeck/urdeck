`urdeck.widgets.steam` shows [Steam](/docs/steam/) on the deck. The top of the card is you: a picture, your name, the game
you are playing and how many friends are playing. Below it, the friends who are online, and at the largest size the games you
played lately.


The card works without connecting Steam: it shows your name, the game, its art and any download, all from your PC. The friends
need one sign-in with the Steam app (see [connecting Steam](/docs/steam/#connecting-steam)). Until then the friends area says
`Connect Steam in Settings`.

## Settings

In the editor, add the widget on **Pages** and select it: the pane on the right shows these settings with a control for each.

## What it shows

- **At 1x1:** the picture with a small mark for your state, and below it the number of friends playing (your name before you
  connect).
- **At 2x1:** the picture, your name, a second line and `N playing · M online`. The second line is the game you are playing,
  or your state (Online, Away, Busy), or `Steam is not running`. While a game downloads or updates and **Show downloads** is
  on, it is the game and its progress, `Ember Court  64%`, with a progress bar under the lines.
- **At 2x2 and 4x2:** the same card, and below it the friends: a picture, a name, and the game they are in (or, for a friend not
  in a game, how they are: Away, Busy). As many whole rows as fit.
- **At 4x4:** as 4x2, with **Recent games** below the friends when **Show recent games** is on: the game's art, the hours of
  the last two weeks and the name.
- **The picture** is the game's art while you play, otherwise your avatar when Steam is connected, otherwise the theme's Steam
  glyph. The mark on it is the theme's colour for your state: the accent while you play, the theme's *good* colour for online,
  *warning* for away or snooze, *critical* for busy, muted when Steam is off.
- **Friends** shows who is online (those playing first) or, with **Friends shown** on *Playing*, only those in a game.
- **A list with nobody in it** says `No friends online` or `Nobody is playing`. A list Steam could not refresh keeps its rows,
  drawn faded, as everywhere in UrDeck.

## Touch

A tap on the card opens the **friends list in the Steam client**. A tap on a friend opens that friend's **profile**, and a tap
on a recent game opens it in your **library**. The taps go to Steam on your PC through Windows, as a
[shortcut](/docs/widgets/shortcut/) does; the widget sends nothing to Steam's servers.

## Looks

The widget draws no card and names no font: its text, its muted lines, its bar and its state colours come from the theme. See
[themes](/docs/themes/).
