`urdeck.widgets.list` shows a **list reading** as rows. It names no provider: the reading's description says which field is
each row's title, its detail line, its status mark, its picture and its link, so the same widget shows the busiest
processes of the [system provider](/docs/widgets/stats/#the-system-readings) today and a friends list or a set of headlines
from any other provider tomorrow. See [Readings and lists](/docs/readings/) for what a list reading is.

<WidgetSizes widget="list" />

It needs only a reading. A new widget shows the processes using the most CPU:

```json
{ "typeId": "urdeck.widgets.list", "col": 0, "row": 0, "width": 2, "height": 2, "reading": "system:processes/top" }
```

## Settings

In the editor, add the widget on **Pages** and select it: the pane on the right shows these settings with a control for each.
The reading's picker offers list readings only, each marked as a list with its number of rows, and shows the fields a list's
rows hold. The file keys are listed for reference.

### Defaults

`title` follows one rule. **Left out**, it is the list's own name (`Busy processes`). **Any text** is used as written. **An
empty text (`""`)** hides it. The title is drawn on cards two cells tall or more; on a card one cell tall the rows take the room.

## What it shows

- **Rows by role.** The title field is the row's text, the detail field its second line, the status field a short mark before
  it (coloured by the field's levels, or the theme's accent), and the picture field a square the height of the first line. A
  value is formatted as everywhere else in UrDeck: a percentage with its sign, a size with its unit, a time in your clock.
  Marks are aligned, so the titles start in one column.
- **A row without a field** is drawn without that part: a friend who is in no game has no second line.
- **At 1x1** the card shows the title and the number of rows, as any card shows a list that way: three friends playing is
  `3 playing`.
- **At 4x4** the rows are set in the theme's title size, which reads from further away.
- **When there are more rows than fit,** `overflow` decides. `scroll` shows whole rows and a tap on the lower part of the list
  pages forward and a tap on the upper part pages back. `rotate` moves the rows up by one on a timer and takes no taps (a tap
  on a row with a link still opens it). A theme with motion off jumps instead of sliding.
- **`maxRows`** takes at most that many rows from the top of the list, in the order the provider published them.
- **A list with no rows** says what the provider says for it (`No busy processes`, `Nobody is playing`), or `Nothing to show`.
- **A reading that is not there:** while the provider has not answered yet, three placeholder rows hold the place; a list the
  provider could not refresh keeps its rows, drawn faded; a reading that cannot be supplied shows its reason
  (`Connect Steam in Settings`).

## Touch

A tap on a row whose list has a link field opens the link, as a [shortcut](/docs/widgets/shortcut/) does: a web address, a
file, a `steam://` link. A tap anywhere else pages the list when `overflow` is `scroll` and the rows do not fit; otherwise it
does nothing and the card does not show a press.

## Looks

The widget draws no card and names no font: its text, its muted lines and its marks come from the theme. See
[themes](/docs/themes/).
