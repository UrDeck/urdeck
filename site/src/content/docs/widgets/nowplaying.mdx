`urdeck.widgets.nowplaying` is a 4x2 card that shows what Windows says is playing: the cover, the title, the artist and
the album, a bar for how far along the track is, and three buttons: previous, play or pause, and next. It follows the same
player the keyboard's media keys control (Windows calls it the current media session), so it works with a music player, a
video in a browser tab or any other application that tells Windows what it plays. It needs no account and no settings.

## Touch

Each of the three buttons is a tap zone of its own, and the zones are as large as the card allows. A tap on a button sends
the command to the player; the card changes when the player says it did, not before, so what you see is always what the
player reports. A finger that lands a little off, in the gap between two buttons, counts for the nearer one. A tap anywhere
else, on the cover or the title, does nothing. A button the player does not accept (a stream that cannot go back) is drawn
dimmed and ignores taps. While your finger is down the whole card shrinks and dims slightly, as for every card (the amounts
are the theme's `press` values).

## What it shows

- **Cover:** the picture the player gives Windows. A player that gives none, or a track without one, shows the theme's music
  glyph. A new track never shows the previous track's cover.
- **Title, artist, album:** each on one line, shrunk to fit its width. A player that reports no artist or album leaves the
  line out.
- **Progress:** the bar moves from the position the player last reported and the time since; the card is drawn again once a
  second while something plays and the length is known, and not at all when it is paused or nothing is playing. A stream
  that reports no length has no bar. A player that does not report its position often (some browsers) may drift until its
  next report, and a player at a different speed is not corrected for.
- **Nothing playing:** the music glyph and `idleLabel`, with no bar and no buttons. This is a calm state, not an error.

The buttons use the theme's `media/play`, `media/pause`, `media/previous` and `media/next` glyphs
([icon sets](/docs/themes/icons/)); a theme's own set that omits them gets the built-in ones, and the card falls back to the
words Prev, Play, Pause and Next only if no set has them.

## What it reads and where the cover goes

The card reads what other applications publish to Windows about their playback, through the `media` data provider that
ships with UrDeck. Nothing is sent anywhere. The cover is written to a small folder of its own under your temporary folder
(`%TEMP%\UrDeck\media-art`), at most two pictures at a time, cleared when UrDeck starts and deleted when it stops. The title,
the artist and the cover are never written to `urdeck.log`. The commands go to the application Windows calls current, which is
what the media keys do. See the [privacy page](/privacy/).

Other widgets can read the same data: the provider's readings are `media:status`, `media:title`, `media:artist`,
`media:album`, `media:app`, `media:position`, `media:position/at`, `media:duration`, `media:art` and `media:can/previous`,
`media:can/next` and `media:can/playpause`.
